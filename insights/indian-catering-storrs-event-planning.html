Indian Catering in Storrs, CT: Planning a Great Event Menu

For an Indian food gathering, the menu is only one part of the planning. Headcount, dietary preferences, dish variety, serving style and timing all affect the final experience.

Start with the guest count

Confirm the approximate number of guests before selecting the menu. It helps determine portions, variety and the right service approach.

Balance familiar favourites with variety

A practical spread can combine rice, breads, gravies, vegetarian dishes and selected meat dishes. Ask guests about dietary preferences before finalizing the menu.

Choose North Indian, South Indian or a mix

Different occasions call for different menus. A focused regional menu can create a coherent meal, while a mixed menu can work well for larger groups with varied preferences.

Plan ahead

For a catered event, confirm the date, headcount, menu and delivery or serving expectations early. Dastarkhawn currently asks for advance ordering and has a minimum-order policy, so confirm the current terms when enquiring.
